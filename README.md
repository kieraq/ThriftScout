# ThriftScout
An AI-powered pricing assistant that helps resellers price clothing and see what they actually take home after fees and shipping.

## The problem
My first depop sale lost me about $52. I bought a Windsor homecoming dress for around $60, sold it for $10, and kept about $8 after fees and the shipping label. I did not know what similar dresses sold for, or how much fees and shipping would take. 

## What will ThriftScout do?

- Compares your item with similar listings across resale apps
- Recommends a quick-sale, recommended, and max price
- Shows what you would keep after fees and shipping on Depop, Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, and Vinted
- Sets a "don't sell below" price so you never sell at a loss
